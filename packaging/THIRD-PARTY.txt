Qt 6 (https://www.qt.io/)
  GNU Lesser General Public License v3: licenses/LGPL-3.0.txt, which

  and FFmpeg libraries (LGPL 2.1 or later): https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

libopenmpt (https://lib.openmpt.org/)
  BSD 3-Clause license: licenses/libopenmpt.txt.
  The Linux and macOS packages build it from the official source release
  with its bundled miniz (MIT), minimp3 (CC0) and stb_vorbis (MIT or public
  domain): licenses/miniz.txt, minimp3.txt, stb_vorbis.txt. The Windows
  package uses the official DLLs, which include mpg123 (LGPL 2.1), libogg,
  libvorbis (BSD) and zlib: licenses/License.*.txt.

  msvcp140*.dll, vcruntime140*.dll: redistributable files of Microsoft
